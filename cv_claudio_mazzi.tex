\section{Research Profile \& Core Methodological Expertise}
Physicist by training and Postdoctoral Researcher in Artificial Intelligence and Computational Statistics. Research focuses on the development of advanced statistical learning, machine learning, and AI methodologies for high-dimensional, structured, and non-Euclidean data, with applications spanning privacy-preserving synthetic data generation, uncertainty quantification, and clinical/health decision-support systems.
\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Privacy-Preserving AI \& Synthetic Data:} Differential Privacy (DP), intrinsic Riemannian manifold statistics, and functional synthetic data generation from single or sparse trajectories.
    \item \textbf{Functional Data Analysis \& Uncertainty Quantification:} Reproducing Kernel Hilbert Space (RKHS) theory, non-stationary kernel engines, orthonormal splinet expansions, and conformal prediction bands for complex stochastic processes ($N = 1$).
    \item \textbf{Machine Learning \& Explainable AI in Healthcare:} Predictive risk stratification, disease complication forecasting in chronic populations (Type-2 Diabetes), NLP-driven classification of clinical risk and workplace aggression in health services.
    \item \textbf{Actuarial Statistics \& Health Systems Benchmarking:} Stochastic loss-reserving methodologies for medical malpractice liabilities and systematic performance auditing across regional administrative health records.
\end{itemize}
