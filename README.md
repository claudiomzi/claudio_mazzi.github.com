## 🔬 Research Profile & Core Methodological Expertise

Physicist by training and Postdoctoral Researcher in Artificial Intelligence and Computational Statistics. My research focuses on the development of advanced statistical learning, machine learning, and AI methodologies for high-dimensional, structured, and non-Euclidean data:

* **Differential Privacy & Riemannian Statistics:** Intrinsic footpoint-invariant Riemannian cross-covariance and correlation metrics across nonlinear manifolds (spheres, SPD matrices under AIRM, Kendall's shape space) and formal Differential Privacy (DP) guarantees on non-Euclidean geometries *(with Carlos J. Soto and Roberto Molinari)*.
* **Functional Data Analysis & Uncertainty Quantification ($N=1$):** Reproducing Kernel Hilbert Space (RKHS) theory, Base-Modulated Non-Stationary (BMNS) covariance kernels, orthonormal splinet basis expansions, and non-asymptotic simultaneous conformal prediction bands for single-trajectory stochastic systems *(with Jacopo Di Iorio and Roberto Molinari)*.
* **Machine Learning & Explainable AI in Medicine:** Predictive risk stratification, micro/macro-vascular disease complication forecasting in chronic cohorts (Type-2 Diabetes), stochastic conformance checking in diabetic foot pathways, and NLP-driven classification of clinical risk and workplace aggression *(with Prof. Chiara Seghieri)*.
* **Actuarial Reserving & Health Systems Auditing:** Deployable stochastic loss-reserving methodologies for medical malpractice liabilities (formally adopted by the Tuscany Regional Health Authority - *Regione Toscana*) and quality benchmarking across 140+ indicators in regional administrative health records.
