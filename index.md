## 🔬 Research Profile & Core Methodological Expertise

Physicist by training and doctoral candidate in Artificial Intelligence for Society. Research focuses on the development and application of machine learning, explainable artificial intelligence (XAI), and computational statistics to solve critical challenges in healthcare systems.

* **Machine Learning & Explainable AI in Medicine:** Predictive risk stratification, disease complication forecasting in chronic populations (Type-2 Diabetes), NLP-driven classification of clinical risk and workplace violence in healthcare services, and model interpretability.
* **Actuarial Statistics & Stochastic Reserving:** Stochastic loss-reserving methodologies for medical malpractice liabilities and claims estimation in public healthcare systems.
* **Functional Data Analysis:** Synthetic functional data generation for data augmentation and privacy-preserving systems.
* **Health Systems Performance Benchmarking:** Data pipeline engineering, statistical auditing, and quality benchmarking across administrative health records.
