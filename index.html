Research Core & Methodological Program

Differential Privacy & Riemannian Statistics

Developing footpoint-invariant Riemannian cross-covariance and correlation metrics for manifold-valued random objects (spheres, SPD matrices, Kendall's shape space) and formal Differential Privacy mechanisms on non-Euclidean geometries, in collaboration with Carlos J. Soto and Roberto Molinari.

Differential Privacy Riemannian Manifolds AIRM Kendall Shape

Functional Uncertainty Quantification ($N=1$)

Statistical inference and exact synthetic trajectory generation for strictly unique, non-stationary stochastic processes ($N=1$). Utilizing Base-Modulated Non-Stationary (BMNS) kernels, orthonormal splinet expansions, and simultaneous conformal prediction bands for climate and longitudinal dynamics.

FDA RKHS Splinets Conformal Prediction N=1 Systems

Clinical ML & Healthcare Decision Support

Machine learning and Explainable AI (XAI) applied to multi-regional administrative cohorts (PRIN RETIN) to forecast micro/macro-vascular complications in Type-2 Diabetes, stochastic conformance checking in diabetic foot care, and NLP for workplace violence classification, with Prof. Chiara Seghieri.

Healthcare AI Explainable AI Type-2 Diabetes NLP Clinical Pathways

Actuarial Reserving & Health Systems Auditing

Deployable stochastic loss-reserving methodologies for medical malpractice liabilities (adopted by Tuscany Regional Health Authority - Regione Toscana) and statistical quality auditing over 140+ performance indicators across regional health systems (MeS System).
